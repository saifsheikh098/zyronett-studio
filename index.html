FAQs

How does the free sample work?

Send your Instagram link and a few raw clips on WhatsApp. We cut a short sample reel. If you like it, we start. If not, you owe nothing.

Who edits my reels?

A small team of editors we work with directly. Every reel goes through the same grading and pacing checks before it reaches you.

How long does a reel take?

24 to 48 hours after we receive your footage. Priority plans get 24-hour delivery.

Do I need to shoot anything special?

No. Shoot with your phone or camera as you normally do. Send the raw clips and we handle the edit, grade, sound and captions.

Can I use the reels on YouTube and Facebook too?

Yes. The reels are yours to post anywhere.

How do we communicate?

On WhatsApp. You send clips and feedback there, and we deliver there too.

My needs are different from these plans. Can you help?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with what you need and we will quote a custom plan.
